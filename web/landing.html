How it picks

These are Sameer's rules for his own weekends. Friends get the same list, filtered by where they can go.

Where it looks
Unstop, Devpost, Devfolio and MLH, checked every six hours.
What it keeps
Registration still open and a cash prize named on the listing. In person only within India; from abroad, only events you can join online.
What it keeps apart
Ideathons, where you pitch an idea without building it, and promptathons, where you compete on prompts. The sites file both under hackathons; here they get their own lists and stay out of the email unless you ask for them.
How it scores
Out of 100, from five parts. Registrations and prizes count on a log scale, so 2,000 sign-ups beats 200 by a lot and 20,000 beats 2,000 by less.

The email

When

Online events, plus in-person ones in the part of India you pick.
Cost
